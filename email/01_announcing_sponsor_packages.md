# A First Big Update

Nine months until HeartConf. A full term. It feels like a lifetime away, but
snap your fingers and another month has passed. Last June I took the train up to
Mechelen to see the Lamot Congress and Heritage Center with my own eyes. Two
weeks later the contract was signed, and the real work to make HeartConf a
reality could begin.

A lot has been done already. After confirming the venue, the next step was to
develop a fresh house brand. HeartConf builds on the legacy of Heart of Clojure,
but has a new name, venue, and scope. Like a Pokémon evolution, it's bigger and
better, while still sharing clear similarities with its predecessor.

The brand style for HeartConf 2027 is uncompromisingly bold. Thick lines, bright
mustard yellow, hot pink that jumps off the page. It reminds us that technology
can still be fun and exciting, welcoming and inspiring. It's also meant to
remind us that, at the end of the day, technology is _human_. Technology is
tekne, it is art. It is humans making choices, to the delight, or the detriment,
of others. We have so much we need to talk about, which is why we are bringing
people together under one roof, for two days of talks, dicussion, and genuine
connection.

## A New ~~Hope~~ Home 

The first edition of Heart of Clojure took place at Hal 5 in Leuven, an old
railway workshop hall turned community and event venue. The second edition we
added Het Depot, an old cinema turned concert hall. The extra real estate
allowed us to have a dedicated community space, our _reified hallway track_,
while giving our speakers a proper stage to shine.

For HeartConf we followed the river Dijle to Mechelen, a charming Belgian city
which in the 17th century served as capital of the United Netherlands. For two
days we'll make ourselves at home at Lamot, a former brewery which is now a
fully decked out congress center. It's a major upgrade, while retaining the old
industrial charm, in a picturesque, historic setting.

Lamot has no fewer than nine different spaces at our disposal. A beautiful
auditorium. A spatious bar area where we'll house sponsors and hold breaks.
There are two more big spaces, one of which will be the Reified Hallway Track,
the other we'll use to host a second conference track. [Take the 3D tour]() to
explore the venue yourself!

So far we have one of the other spaces earmarked for a Quiet Room, a space with
the vibes of a library. No screens, hushed voices, the ideal place to decompress
if you're feeling overstimulated. There are some ideas of what to do with the
others, but nothing final yet. We'll certainly revisit this topic in future
newsletters!

commit early count double. They give us the budgettary headroom to truly make
this an extraordinary event. It allows us to spend more on the finer things in
life, like food and drink, but it also means we can consider bringing in
speakers from further afield, or with a bigger public profile. Sponsoring allows
us to record talks and offer them online, free of charge.

moving to Lamot is that we have much more space. This means a lot more

If you or your company is considering sponsoring then do [get in

If you're not in a position to sponsor yourself, but you have a warm contact
