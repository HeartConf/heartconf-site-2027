A First Big Update

August still feels ages away, but organising a conference is a long process. We’ll be grateful in the hectic months leading up to the conference for all the groundwork we’re doing right now.

Our Home in the World, and on the Web

The first step is to secure the venue. This is the canvas upon which we will create an awesome event. I visited the Lamot conference center last June and immediately knew it would make a great home for HeartConf. It’s a beautiful venue — a mix of old industrial charm and modern architecture — in the middle of the city of Mechelen, surrounding by charming bars and pedestrian areas.

Mechelen is just a short trainride away from Leuven, where we held Heart of Clojure. Like Leuven it has a rich history, which is reflected in the cityscape. And like Leuven it’s just the right size. Big enough to be lively with plenty on offer to have an interesting stay, but small enough to feel cozy and easy to navigate.

Once we had a venue and a date, the real work starts. In August I worked out the house style for this event, and put together the HeartConf 2027 website. It ended up being a bold design with bright sunny yellow, reflecting both sunny summer vibes, and the boldness and optimism that inspire our choice of topics. Our heart logo has been painted a hot pink, highlighting both the humanistic nature of HeartConf, and the diversity in our community.

Looking for Sponsors

Now it’s time for the next big step, opening the search for sponsors. We’ve put together a number of attractive packages. One of the great things about moving to Lamot is that we have much more space. That gives us a lot of possibilities for programming, like a second track or other parallel activities like workshops.

But it also means we have much more attractive floor real estate to offer to sponsors. This was always a bit of a challenge at the Heart of Clojure venue, where space was a bit more tight.

So we’ve revamped our sponsor packages substantially. If you or your company is interested in sponsoring then get in touch!.

If you’re not in a position to sponsor yourself, but you have a warm contact that you can introduce us to, then please do get in touch as well.

Next steps

The sponsor search will likely continue well into next year, but in the meanwhile we are working on the next pieces of the puzzle. We hope to get tickets up for sale soon, and after that we’ll launch the CFP, likely some time in November.
