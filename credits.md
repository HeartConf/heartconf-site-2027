# Credits

Many people have contributed to this creative endeavor, and we gratefully make
use of online resources that others have made available. We try to give credit
where credit is due.

The iconic heart-shaped logo was originally created by [Lubov Soltan](https://www.behance.net/lubovsoltan) for Heart of Clojure 2019. <svg style="display:inline" height="0.80em" viewBox="0 0 100 100"><use href="#svg-hc-logo"/></svg>

For copy and headings we use [Outfit](https://github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts) by [Rodrigo Fuenzalida](https://www.behance.net/erreefe).
