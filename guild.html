HeartConf Guild

It takes a village to create a conference. HeartConf Guild members are the people most closely connected to the project. They are privy to internal information, and get to have a say in the direction we go in.

This is a low traffic mailing list primarily meant to announce Guild Meets, open calls every few weeks, where we share updates about the conference, and discuss planning and organisation.

Joining this mailing list does not come with any expectations about your involvement. Just come and listen if you're curious, and if you're keen to jump in then we can figure it out from there.

If you are here that normally means you received this link from an existing Guild member, since membership is by invitation. If that's not the case but you're curious and would like to come and see what it's about, just send us an email and tell us a bit more about yourself!
